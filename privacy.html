Privacy policy

Soko Moving Co. · Updated September 25, 2026

Game progress

Your completed puzzles, current level and best results are stored on your device. When available, Apple iCloud synchronizes progress across your devices. We do not receive your game progress on a developer-operated server.

Optional tips

Every level and game feature is free. You may make an optional, repeatable one-time tip using Apple's in-app purchase system. Tips do not unlock content and are not subscriptions.

We use RevenueCat to display the tip jar and process purchase status. RevenueCat processes an anonymous app user identifier, purchase and transaction information, and technical information needed to provide and troubleshoot its service, such as app, SDK and operating-system versions. Apple handles payment details; we do not receive your card information.

Read RevenueCat's privacy policy and Apple's privacy policy.

No advertising or tracking

The app does not include advertising or cross-app tracking. No account is required to play or tip.

Support and privacy requests

If you contact us, we use your message and contact details to respond. For support or privacy questions, contact manyoneek@icloud.com. Device progress can be removed by deleting the app; iCloud data is managed through your Apple account. Purchase records may be retained as required to operate and verify purchases.
